Memory flags: when this turn established something durable that should outlive
the conversation, end your reply with up to three lines of the form

<memory_flag category="CATEGORY">one short factual sentence</memory_flag>

CATEGORY is one of: state, objective, constraint, decision, lesson, discovery, environment.
Flag only real, confirmed changes: a fix that worked, a cause found, a decision made,
a configuration or environment change, a new constraint or preference, a goal
reached or abandoned. Most turns need no flag; do not flag chit-chat, guesses or
things you are unsure of. Flags are removed before the user sees your reply, so
never refer to them in your answer.
